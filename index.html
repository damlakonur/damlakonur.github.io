Hi! I'm Damla

I'm interested in how we can reconstruct, represent, and understand the three-dimensional world.

I'm an M.Sc. student in Computer Science at the Technical University of Munich. My interests lie in 3D computer vision, particularly 3D scene representations, world models, and dynamic reconstruction.

Alongside my studies, I work as a Computer Vision Engineer at ImFusion, deploying and evaluating computer vision models for medical imaging and contributing to research on 2D and 3D point tracking.

Previously, I completed a double major in Computer Engineering and Control and Automation Engineering at Istanbul Technical University. My experience spans 3D reconstruction, generative models, and software engineering in C++ and Python.
